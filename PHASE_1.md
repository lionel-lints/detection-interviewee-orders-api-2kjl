# Phase 1

This task comes in two phases. **Do not open `PHASE_2.md` until you have finished phase 1 and told
the interviewer you are done with it.** Phase 2 builds on phase 1; reading ahead will not help you
and will make the exercise less useful for both of us.

Fulfilment needs to know about orders as they are created, not the next morning.

Right now they get a nightly CSV drop, which means an order placed at 09:00 does not get fulfilled
until the following day. The fulfilment team wants to consume order creations the same way they
already consume cancellations.

Build it.

The invariants in the README are the bar. You are expected to use whatever AI tooling you normally
use — that is the point, not a concession.
